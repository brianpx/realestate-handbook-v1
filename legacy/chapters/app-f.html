Appendix F: Essential Links

Trusted sources to bookmark. Each link opens in a new tab. Rules and forms vary by state, so confirm anything legal with your broker.

ResourceWhat you will find
California Association of REALTORS® (C.A.R.) (opens in a new tab)State association: standard forms, legal hotline, market data and member resources.
National Association of REALTORS® (NAR) (opens in a new tab)National association: research, advocacy and professional resources.
NAR Code of Ethics (opens in a new tab)The ethical standards REALTOR® members agree to follow.
California Department of Real Estate (DRE) (opens in a new tab)Licensing, exams, renewals and the rules your license depends on. Look up your own state's regulator if you work elsewhere.
ARELLO (opens in a new tab)Find the real estate regulator for any U.S. state or Canadian province.
HUD Fair Housing (opens in a new tab)Federal Fair Housing Act guidance, protected classes and how complaints work.
Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (opens in a new tab)Plain-language guides to mortgages, closing costs and your clients' rights.
National Do Not Call Registry (opens in a new tab)Check and understand the rules before you cold call or text prospects.
IRS: Small Business and Self-Employed (opens in a new tab)Estimated taxes, deductions and record keeping for independent contractors.
