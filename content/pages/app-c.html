Appendix C: CRM setup checklist

Finish every item in your first week. Ask your office technology trainer for help with anything that takes more than 30 minutes. The groups below follow the Chapter 4 setup, in the same order. Items that name a Keller Williams Command screen are labeled as examples; in another CRM, use its equivalent. One plan in the third group is built now, ahead of any contract. The post-close plan is built once, assigned on the day the client goes under contract, and activated on closing day; it runs seven dated touches in the first year, starting with the closing-day note. The last group carries the gate from Chapter 3. Before you pay for leads, pass the 9 p.m. test: a lead arriving at 9 p.m. tonight gets an immediate automated reply, a call by the next morning and a 90-day plan, all without you having to remember.
