Appendix B: Follow-up plan starter kit

Seven plans to build into your CRM. The first four take one afternoon; B.5 to B.7 wait until you have a farm, a not-yet buyer or a valuation lead to put on them. Write every message in your own voice before you turn the plan on.

B.1 New online lead (90 days)

Send automated texts or calls only to people whose consent you have recorded in the CRM, and stop at the first opt-out. Honor quiet hours. An inquiry is not, by itself, permission for this sequence. The day-0 text waits until you have that permission.

B.4 Post-close plan (first year)

Use Table 10.1 in Chapter 10. Six steps: closing day, one week, 30 days, quarterly (months 3, 6 and 9), home anniversary (month 12), and the annual appreciation event or gift. That is seven dated touches per client in the first year. After the first year, the contact stays on the quarterly plan in B.3. The Home Anniversary plan in Table 5.1 is not a separate sequence: the first anniversary is the month-12 step of Table 10.1, and every later one is the annual touch in B.3.

B.5 Monthly Neighborhood Nurture (ongoing)

For a geographic farm. Every household hears from you once a month; the cycle repeats through the year.

Table B.5. Monthly Neighborhood Nurture (ongoing)
MONTHTOUCHCONTENT
1MailNeighborhood market snapshot: sales, days on market, one price trend.
2Email or social postA local story: a business opening, school or park news, a seasonal event.
3MailJust-listed or just-sold card for a home in the farm (Chapter 3).
Repeat every quarter. Add an open-house invitation whenever you have a listing in the farm.
