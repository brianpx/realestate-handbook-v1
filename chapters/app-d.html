Appendix D: Glossary

8x8
A SmartPlan of eight touches over eight weeks, used to establish a new relationship with a sphere contact.
Agency disclosure
The required explanation, usually on a state form, of whom the agent represents in a transaction and what that means for the client.
Command
Keller Williams' agent platform for contacts, tasks, SmartPlans, Opportunities, marketing and transactions. Accessed at agent.kw.com and through the Command mobile app.
Conversation
In this handbook, a two-way exchange about real estate with someone who could buy, sell or refer. Voicemails and unanswered texts do not count.
Cost per closed deal
Money and time spent on a lead source divided by the closings it produced. The number that decides where your budget goes.
Expired listing
A property whose listing agreement ended without a sale. Owners are often still motivated to sell.
Fair Housing
Federal, state and local laws that prohibit discrimination in housing on the basis of protected characteristics.
Farming
Concentrated, consistent marketing to one geographic neighborhood with the goal of becoming its recognized agent.
FSBO
For sale by owner. A property the owner is attempting to sell without an agent.
IDX
Internet Data Exchange. The feed that lets your website display MLS listings and capture search leads.
KW App
The Keller Williams consumer app, branded to you, that clients use to search for homes.
Lagging indicator
A result that reports what already happened, such as closings or income.
Leading indicator
An activity you control today that predicts future results, such as conversations and appointments.
Lead routing
Rules in Command that decide which team member receives a new lead.
Market Center
A Keller Williams office. Home of your team leader, productivity coach, training and accountability.
Nurture
The scheduled, multi-touch follow-up that keeps a lead engaged until they are ready to act.
Opportunities
The pipeline in Command where each potential transaction moves through stages from new to closed.
Pipeline value
The sum of likely commission across active Opportunities. A weekly scorecard number.
SmartPlan
An automated sequence of touches in Command: texts, emails, and tasks that remind you to call.
Speed-to-lead
The time between a lead's inquiry and your first real response. Minutes win; hours lose.
Sphere of influence
Everyone who already knows you and would take your call. Your highest-converting lead source.
Touch
Any scheduled contact with a lead or client: call, text, email, note, visit or value piece.
Value piece
A touch that gives something useful (a report, a guide, an introduction) rather than asking for something.
