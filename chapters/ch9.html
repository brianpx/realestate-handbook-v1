PART VI: Serve and Keep

9. Working with clients and dealing with objections

The Consultation Sequence: 1. Frame ➔ 2. Ask ➔ 3. Listen ➔ 4. Present ➔ 5. Agree.

The 4-Step Objection Pattern

  1. Acknowledge. "That makes sense."
  2. Clarify. "Can I ask what's behind that?"
  3. Respond. Answer with facts or data.
  4. Check. "Does that address it?" (Then stop talking).
OBJECTIONRESPONSE FRAMEWORKGOAL
"I'm just looking."Acknowledge & offer value: "Would it help if I sent a few options matching your description?"Keep conversation open.
"We're not ready yet."Explore timeline: "When do you think you might be ready? I can send occasional updates."Qualify timing & earn nurture right.
"I already have an agent."Respect & stay open: "Great. If anything changes or you'd like a second opinion, reach out."Stay professional & memorable.
"Your commission is too high."Focus on net proceeds: "Let's look at what you actually net after marketing and negotiation."Shift fee to outcome.
"I want to wait for the market to improve."Provide data: "Here is what similar homes have done recently... let's compare costs."Educate without pressure.
"I need to think about it."Clarify: "Of course. What specifically would you like to think through?"Uncover real objection.
"Can you just send me listings?"Agree + next step: "I'll send a few. What's the best way to follow up after you look?"Move passive to engaged.
"You're new, aren't you?"Honest & specific: "I am, and that means you get my full attention backed by my Market Center."Replace worry with evidence.
"We want to interview other agents."Support & set follow-up: "That's smart. What will you compare? I'll follow up Thursday."Stay in running with next step.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • Frame, ask, listen, present, agree. Asking/listening take half the meeting.
  • Objections are requests for information. Acknowledge, clarify, respond, check.
  • Document every conversation in Command the same day.
