PART IV: Nurture

5. Nurturing leads

Most leads convert between the fifth and twelfth touch. Most agents quit after the second. The gap between those two facts is where your business is.

In this chapter you will learn to

  • Explain why multi-touch follow-up works and plan 8 to 12 touches over 90 days.
  • Build SmartPlans that mix calls, texts, emails and value pieces.
  • Qualify a lead early with four questions.
  • Run a weekly pipeline review so nobody falls through the cracks.

Why one call is never enough

Online leads are usually months from a decision. 80% of conversions occur between touches 5 and 12.

Designing a nurture plan

Properties of a good plan: Front-loaded (4-5 touches in first 2 weeks), Mixed channel (calls, texts, emails), Value every third touch (give before asking), and Long (at least 90 days before moving to quarterly plan).

The weekly pipeline review

Every Friday, review your active contacts and ask:
1. When did I last have a real two-way conversation with this person?
2. What is the next step, and is it scheduled?
3. Is this person in the right stage?

Anyone with no conversation in 14 days and no scheduled step gets a call Monday.

● TRY IT TODAY
• Build the new-lead SmartPlan from Appendix B in Command and apply it to leads from the last 30 days.
• Call the 5 oldest leads in your database that never got a plan. Ask the 4 qualifying questions.
• Put a recurring 30-minute "Pipeline review" block on Friday afternoons.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • Most conversions happen between touch 5 and touch 12. Plan for at least 90 days.
  • Front-load touches, mix channels, give value every third touch.
  • Qualify early on timeline, motivation, financing and representation.
  • Review pipeline every Friday. Fourteen quiet days triggers a call.
