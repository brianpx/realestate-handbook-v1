12. Daily, weekly and monthly execution plans

Figure 7. First 90 Days Milestone Roadmap

• Days 1 to 30 (Install System): Command setup, contacts tagged, SmartPlans live, 50 sphere conversations, 1 open house, speed-to-lead tested.
• Days 31 to 60 (Build Rhythm): Morning blocks held 4/5 days, 15-25 conversations/wk, first consultations & buyer agreement signed.
• Days 61 to 90 (Grow Pipeline): 5+ active opportunities, 1st contract written, post-close plan built, lead sources evaluated.

Weekly & Monthly Measures

Track four numbers every Friday: Conversations held, Appointments set, Follow-ups completed, and Pipeline value. Spend one hour on the first working day of each month reviewing source conversion and pipeline flow.
