12. Daily, weekly and monthly execution plans

Ninety days is long enough to build the system and start a pipeline, and short enough to hold yourself to.

In this chapter you will learn to

  • Follow a 90-day roadmap with milestones at days 30, 60 and 90.
  • Measure the same four numbers every week.
  • Run a monthly review that adjusts where your time goes.

Days 1 to 30: install the system

Finish CRM setup (Chapter 4), import and tag contacts, launch the basic follow-up plans, and have at least 50 real conversations with your sphere. Hold one open house or one networking event. Run the speed-to-lead test. Do not worry about closings; worry about whether the system is on.

Days 31 to 60: build the rhythm

Hold the morning block at least four days out of five. Hit 15 to 25 conversations a week and 3 to 6 appointments set (Table 8.2). Review your numbers every Friday. By day 60 you should have held your first consultations and signed your first buyer representation agreement. The content calendar from Chapter 3 should be running on its own.

Days 61 to 90: grow the pipeline

Your pipeline review should show at least five active opportunities. Write your first contract. Build the post-close plan before you need it. Count the results by lead source and decide, with data, whether paid leads make sense next quarter. Record your conversion ratios so the next 90 days can be planned from real numbers.

Measure every week

Four numbers, every Friday, written down. Table 8.2 sets the targets and Appendix A is the scorecard.

  • Conversations held (two-way, about real estate, with a possible buyer, seller or referrer). Outbound calls are the input; count them too.
  • Appointments set (consultations and listing presentations on the calendar).
  • Follow-ups completed (follow-up plan tasks cleared on time).
  • Pipeline value (sum of likely commission across active Opportunities).

Review every month

On the first working day of the month, spend one hour on four questions: Which source produced the most appointments per hour spent? Which follow-up plan got the most replies? Where did leads stall in the pipeline? What one change will I make this month? Write the answers in your CRM as a note to yourself so next month's review can check them.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  1. Install, build rhythm, grow pipeline. Thirty days each.
  2. Four weekly numbers: conversations, appointments, follow-ups, pipeline value.
  3. One monthly review decides where your time goes next.
