Appendix B: Follow-up plan starter kit

Four plans to build into your CRM in one afternoon. Write every message in your own voice before you turn the plan on.

B.1 New online lead (90 days)

Get permission before an automated marketing text, honor quiet hours, and stop on the first opt-out. An inquiry is not, by itself, permission for this sequence. The day-0 text waits until you have that permission.
