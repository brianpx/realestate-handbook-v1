PART V: Stay on Track

8. Staying organized and motivated

Organization reduces stress, and lower stress protects motivation. Both come from the same place: one source of truth and a calendar that is already decided.

In this chapter you will learn to

  • Time-block a week so that lead generation happens before anything else.
  • Use a daily structure that opens and closes in Command.
  • Protect motivation by measuring leading indicators and reviewing every Friday.

One source of truth

Every contact, note, task and opportunity lives in Command. Not some of them. Not the important ones. All of them. The moment you keep a second list, you have two places to check and one of them will be wrong. If a conversation happened and it is not in Command, treat it as not having happened: log it now.

A sample day

  • Morning (first 2 to 3 hours). Review the Command dashboard. Clear overnight SmartPlan tasks. Make follow-up calls, then prospecting calls, until you hit your daily conversation number.
  • Midday. Appointments, showings, listing previews, or more prospecting if the calendar is empty.
  • Afternoon. Client work: offers, listing preparation, marketing, additional follow-up.
  • End of day (15 minutes). Update Command. Move Opportunities. Write tomorrow's top three priorities.

The morning block is sacred because it is the only time of day that nobody else wants. Clients, lenders and inspectors will all ask for afternoons. Give them afternoons.

Motivation that lasts

Motivation fails when you measure the wrong thing. If the only scoreboard is closings, you will go 60 to 90 days with a score of zero, and nobody stays motivated through that. Keep a scoreboard of leading indicators instead: calls, conversations, follow-ups completed, appointments set. Those numbers move every day you do the work. Table 8.2 sets the targets and Appendix A is the scorecard.

Three habits protect motivation:

  • Celebrate small wins. A booked appointment is a win. Ten conversations before noon is a win. Say it out loud or write it down.
  • Review every Friday. Compare this week's numbers to last week's. Decide one thing to do differently. Then stop working on it until Monday.
  • Stay close to the Market Center. Training, accountability partners, team meetings. Real estate is a solo business that is easier with company.
● PITFALL
Letting a slow week become a slow month by "waiting for things to pick up". Nothing picks up on its own. Go back to the morning block and the daily number; the market responds to activity.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  1. Command is the only list. A conversation not logged did not happen.
  2. Lead generation goes first in the day, in the same place every day.
  3. Measure leading indicators, celebrate small wins, review on Fridays, stay connected.
