How to use this handbook

Most new agents do not fail for lack of effort. They fail because effort is scattered across activities that do not compound. This handbook gives you one system with three outcomes, and every chapter serves one of them:

  • Find. Generate a steady flow of conversations with people who may buy or sell.
  • Nurture. Follow up often enough, and helpfully enough, that you are the agent they call when they are ready.
  • Keep. Stay useful after closing so past clients send you the next ones.

The book is written to be worked, not just read. Each chapter opens with what you will be able to do by the end, teaches the idea, shows the words to say, and closes with an exercise and a checkpoint. Read a chapter, do the exercise the same day, and move on. Do not try to install everything in one weekend.

Reading plan

  • Week 1: Chapters 1, 4 and 12. Set up your CRM, protect your calendar, and learn what you will measure.
  • Week 2: Chapters 2, 5 and 6. Start conversations and put every lead on a plan.
  • Week 3: Chapters 7, 8 and 9. Audit your habits and practice consultations.
  • Week 4: Chapters 3, 10, 11 and 13. Add marketing, post-close plans and professional safeguards.
