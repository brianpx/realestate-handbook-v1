# Domain name review

Written 8 November 2026 from a naming pass for this app. No name was chosen and nothing was purchased. Availability and price were read that day from the public Vercel registrar search (`POST https://api.vercel.com/v1/registrar/domains/search`). Every name below that was recommended came back `available: true` at the standard `.com` price: **$12.03 for one year**, renewal **$12.03**, `premium: false`.

A later check can disagree. `available: false` means the name was not offered for registration, or the check could not confirm it. It does not by itself prove who owns it.

The product this was named for is *Finding, Keeping, and Nurturing Clients*: a 90-day handbook for new real estate agents, with tools that turn an income goal into a daily conversation target, set up CRM and follow-up, and support scripts and a weekly review. The public line is "Know what to do each day to build a real estate business." All three passes stayed on `.com`. "Realtor" was avoided because it is a National Association of Realtors mark.

## Pass 1 — the book, said plainly

Ranked for a buyer who has heard the title or the 90-day offer.

| Rank | Domain | Why it was kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | findkeepnurture.com | The title, compressed into something someone can type after hearing it. |
| 2 | the90dayagent.com | Matches "a 90-day handbook for new real estate agents." Easy to say. |
| 3 | newagentdaily.com | Matches the daily-plan promise, including the goal, CRM, and weekly-review tools. |
| 4 | nurtureclients.com | The part of the title that lasts past the first 90 days. |
| 5 | newagent90.com | Shortest clear form: who it is for, and how long the plan runs. |

Preference from that pass: **findkeepnurture.com** if the domain should be the product name. **the90dayagent.com** if it should be easy to say on a call or a card.

Obvious handbook names that were not offered: `clienthandbook.com`, `agenthandbook.com`, `theagenthandbook.com`, `newagenthandbook.com`, `realestatehandbook.com`, `thedailyagent.com`. Also not offered: `findkeep.com`, `findandkeep.com`, `keepclients.com`, `keepingclients.com`, `clientnurture.com`, `clientkeep.com`, `thefirst90.com`, `agent90.com`, `yourfirst90.com`, `firstyearagent.com`, `newagentplan.com`, `newagentbook.com`, `agentplaybook.com`.

## Pass 3 — short, startup-style, broader real estate

Asked for short names that sound like a software product. The association could widen past this handbook to housing, neighborhood, and daily client work. Dictionary words and the usual `-ly` forms (`porch.com`, `stoop.com`, `hearth.com`, `gable.com`, `latch.com`, `foyer.com`, `mews.com`, `porchly.com`, `nestly.com`, and the rest of that pattern) were not offered. Known product names were left alone even as ideas, including Opendoor, DoorLoop, Lofty, Nestio, Close.io, and Compass.

These ten were the ones that stayed pronounceable, spellable after one hearing, and free.

| Rank | Domain | Letters | Why it was kept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | porchel.com | 7 | Porch, coined. Say POR-chel. |
| 2 | mewsio.com | 6 | A mews is a lane of houses. Shortest, and it already sounds like software. Say MEWS-ee-oh. |
| 3 | dayporch.com | 8 | Understood on the first hearing: a daily stop at the front of the house. |
| 4 | veranly.com | 7 | Veranda, trimmed. Say vair-AN-lee. |
| 5 | herthio.com | 7 | Hearth. Say HER-thee-oh. |
| 6 | stoopel.com | 7 | The city version of Porchel. The stoop is the front steps. |
| 7 | onmews.com | 6 | Reads as a phrase: on the mews. |
| 8 | garthio.com | 7 | A garth is an enclosed yard. Say GARTH-ee-oh. |
| 9 | daystoop.com | 8 | The same daily idea as Dayporch, with a neighborhood feel. |
| 10 | warmstoop.com | 9 | A warm place people come back to. |

Preference from that pass: **porchel.com** as the brand, **mewsio.com** as the short one, **dayporch.com** as the one that needs no explanation.

Coins and compounds that were free but set aside as weaker, harder to say, or too easy to mis-hear: `dayhearth.com`, `daygable.com`, `daycurb.com`, `daymews.com`, `daygarth.com`, `warmmews.com`, `warmgarth.com`, `warmlatch.com`, `openmews.com`, `opengarth.com`, `firststoop.com`, `gazebio.com`, `mewsel.com`, `garthel.com`, `cortiles.com`. ## How to use this

Pick one name, then re-check it before anyone pays. The registrar quote above is a snapshot from 8 November 2026, not a hold. After a name is chosen, registration, DNS, and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` are a separate cutover step. This file does not authorize a purchase.
